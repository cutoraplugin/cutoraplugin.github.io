Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-30. Data controller: the Cutora team. Contact: SupportCutora@gmail.com.

The short version

Cutora runs on your computer. Your videos, audio, transcripts and captions are never sent anywhere: not to us, not to a cloud, not to third parties. The plugin has no ads, trackers or analytics. We only receive what a purchase and a license activation need.

This website

The website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or ad trackers. It remembers your language, theme and sound choice in your browser's local storage; this never leaves your device. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (IP address, time, requested page) for security, for a short period.

When you buy

For your order we receive: your email address, what you bought, the amount, the date and the payment status. The payment provider handles your card details; we never see them. We use your email to send the download link and license key and to answer you if you write to support. We keep order data as long as accounting and consumer law require.

When you activate your key

Once, when you enter the key, Cutora sends our license server: the key, an anonymous device identifier (a one-way hash that cannot be turned back into your hardware details) and the plugin version. The server remembers which two devices use the key. Your IP address may appear in server logs for a short period. Nothing else is sent, and after activation Cutora works offline.

When the plugin goes online

Only when you ask it to, and directly to the site involved:

  • getting or updating the video downloader: yt-dlp and Deno are downloaded from their official GitHub pages and checked against their checksums;
  • import by link: the video is downloaded from YouTube, Instagram or TikTok. Those sites see an ordinary request from your computer, and their own policies apply;
  • the footage pack: downloaded from our file storage when you press Download.

What stays on your computer

Settings, statistics, achievements, your styles and curves (in %APPDATA%\Cutora), transcripts, rendered captions and downloaded videos (next to your project). We have no access to any of it.

Who else processes data

The payment provider (payments), our email service (sending your key), our hosting and file storage (the website, the license server, the footage pack). They process data only to provide these services. We don't sell data and don't use your material to train AI models.

Your rights

You can ask what data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, or object to processing. Write to SupportCutora@gmail.com. If you are in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Cutora is not intended for children under 14.
